The problem begins with Ravi, his chai stall near a college. Some days he runs out of milk at 11 am while some days he have to throw away litres. But he has 6 months of daily sales written in his notebook.

Four questions to answer before mob=ving forward:
1. What happened?
2. Why did it happened?
3. What will happen?
4. What should we do now?

Data science is the practice of using this data, statistics and programming to answer the questions like these and support decisions.
AI: machines doing tasks that normally need human intelligence.
Machine learning (ML): a part of AI where the program learns patterns from data instead of following hand-written rules.
Deep learning (DL): a part of ML that uses neural networks with many layers.

The lifecycle that revolves around almost every project moves through these stages, often looping back:
1. Define the problem
2. Collect data
3. Clean data
4. Explore data
5. Model (this is where ML sometimes enters)
6. Communicate results

To answer this as of Ravi:
1. Define the problem: Ravi wants to reduce milk wastage and avoid running out of milk.
2. Collect data: He records daily chai sales and milk usage.
3. Clean data: He checks the records for missing or incorrect information.
4. Explore data: He finds which days have the highest chai sales.
5. Model: He uses average sales to estimate how much milk he needs.
6. Communicate results: He prepares a report to plan his daily milk orders.

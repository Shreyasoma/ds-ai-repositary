TYPES OF MACHINE LEARNING

Lets take another problem, Three people each want to use data:
- A bank has 10,000 past loan applications, each marked “repaid” or “defaulted”. It wants to judge new applicants.
- A shop has purchase records for 5,000 customers with no labels at all. It wants to find natural groups of customers for marketing.
- A robot vacuum has no dataset. It learns by trying routes and getting a reward for covering more floor in less time.

Three types of learning:
1. Supervised learning: the data includes the answer (label) you want to predict. The model learns from the examples with known answers.
2. Unsupervised learning: there are no labels. The model looks for structure (groups, patterns) in the data by itself.
3. Reinforcement learning: an agent takes actions in an environment and learns from rewards and penalties.
